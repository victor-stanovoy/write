The essentials of a focused writing app, plus tools that usually cost extra.

Focus mode

Dim everything except the line, sentence or paragraph you are writing. Typewriter scrolling keeps your eyes in one place.

Syntax highlight

Colour nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs and conjunctions to spot weak phrasing and repeated patterns (English, heuristic).

Live Markdown

Headings, emphasis, quotes, lists, tables, task lists and code blocks are styled as you type. Open a side-by-side preview any time.

Library & outline

All documents in a searchable sidebar, with an automatic outline of your headings for quick navigation.

Version history

Automatic snapshots as you write. Restore an earlier version with one click. Nothing is ever lost.

Export anywhere

Markdown, HTML, PDF, Word (.doc), plain text, or copy as rich text for email and docs.

Command palette

Press Ctrl/⌘ K to do anything: headings, lists, find & replace, themes, export.

Goals & streaks

Set a daily word goal, see your progress in the status bar and keep your writing streak alive.

Themes

Light, dark, sepia or automatic, with your own accent colour, font, size, line height and line width.

Find & replace

Case-sensitive and regular-expression search across the document, with replace-all.

Music, no videos

Play your own Spotify playlists in a small player while you write. Video sites and podcast videos are blocked, so nothing pulls your eyes away.

Dictionary & thesaurus

Select a word to see definitions, synonyms, opposites and rhymes, then click one to swap it into your text.

Sprints & stats

Timed sprints with an optional no-deleting rule, plus a writing calendar with streaks and your best day.

Studio visual editor

Edit your page visually: drag in colors, highlights, images and blocks. Everything is saved straight back into plain Markdown.

50 templates

A gallery for journals, planners, meetings, OKRs, essays, recipes, stories, READMEs and more. Pick one and start writing.

Comments, embeds & styles

Hide private // comments, embed notes with ![[Note]], and export in Manuscript, Magazine or Compact styles.

Style check

Underlines clichés, wordy phrases, repeated words and passive voice as you write, with one-click fixes. Runs on your device.

Tags & smart folders

Add #tags anywhere, filter by them, and save searches like #draft older:7 as smart folders.

Rearrange sections

See every section as a card and drag them into a new order. Subsections travel with their parent.

13 themes

Light, Paper, Sepia, Rose, Lavender, Mint, Solar, Dark, Graphite, Midnight, Nord, Plum and Ink, with eight accent colors.

Present & read

Turn any note into full-screen slides, or switch to a calm reading mode. Plus a daily note and one-click table formatting.

Focused email composer

Write the message in a calm page, press Ready to send, add recipients and subject, then copy every part or open your mail app. Wren never sends mail itself.

Snippets & compare

Type ;date or your own shortcuts to expand text, compare any saved version with your current note, or split a note into one note per section.

Ultra focus

One sentence on a blank screen. Timer, word goal, optional draft mode with no deleting, built-in rain and brown noise, and a calm session summary.

YouTube & Spotify music

Paste a YouTube or Spotify link and it plays in the official player right next to your writing.

Footnotes, targets & prompts

Real [^1] footnotes, a word target with a progress bar for each note, key sounds and writing prompts for when you are stuck.

Wikipedia research

Look things up in a side pane without leaving your document. Quote, link or cite with one click. Includes full-screen mode.

Slash menu

Type / to insert headings, lists, tables, code blocks, callouts and dates without leaving the keyboard.

Linked notes

Connect notes with [[wiki links]], get autocomplete as you type, and see backlinks in the sidebar.

Writing insights

Reading level, passive voice, long sentences, repeated words and weak words like “very” and “just”, highlighted on demand.

Smart typing

Curly quotes, em dashes and ellipses; auto-closing brackets; move lines with Alt ↑↓; Ctrl/⌘ P to jump between notes.

Templates & timer

Start from journal, meeting, blog or weekly-review templates. Run a focus timer and hear your text read aloud.

Trash, pins & backups

Deleted notes stay in Trash for 30 days. Pin favourites, back everything up as JSON, or export all notes as a .zip of Markdown.

Private by design

Documents are stored on your device. No sign-up, no tracking, no cloud. Works offline once loaded.

Open & save files

Import .md and .txt files by dragging them in, or open a file from disk and save straight back to it.

Questions

Where are my documents stored?

On your device only: in the browser’s local storage for the web app, and in the app’s own storage for the Mac app. Export to Markdown at any time for backups, or use “Open file” to edit files on disk directly.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The Mac app is fully offline. The web app caches itself after the first visit and then works without a connection.

Is syntax highlight accurate?

It uses a lightweight English word list and word-ending rules, so it is fast and private but not perfect. Treat it as a hint for spotting patterns, not a grammar checker.

Does Wren play or show videos?

No. Embedded videos are blocked, the music player only accepts Spotify tracks, albums, playlists and artists (not podcasts), and video links in your notes are shown as “video blocked”. Spotify’s player may ask you to log in to Spotify for full songs.

What leaves my device?

Your notes never do. Only the lookups you make are sent: Wikipedia searches, dictionary and thesaurus words, and Spotify’s or YouTube’s own player when you open it.

Do the web app and Mac app share documents?

Not automatically, because nothing is uploaded. Move writing between them by exporting to Markdown or by importing files.
